Last updated: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MI Liquor List (“the App”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal information, and none of it is transmitted to our servers.

a) Locally Stored Data

The App stores the following information locally on your device using platform-provided persistent storage: product codes, inventory counts, and user preferences. This data remains on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

b) Camera Access

The App may request access to your device camera solely for the purpose of scanning product barcodes to look up items in the price book. Camera data is processed in real time on your device. No images, video, or barcode data are captured, stored, transmitted, or shared with any third party.

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use advertising identifiers, device information, and usage data for the purposes of ad personalization, frequency capping, conversion tracking, and fraud prevention. This data is collected and governed by Google’s Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You may opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

  • Google AdMob: As described above, Google’s ad services may collect data independently in accordance with their own privacy policy.

All user data is retained locally on your device for as long as the App is installed. You may delete this data at any time by clearing the App’s data through your device settings or by uninstalling the App. We implement reasonable security measures to protect the App, but we cannot guarantee the security of data stored on your device.
